Method notes

Why the emission factor changes. The electricity the campus draws from KSEB is a blend of power from different sources. The mix of those sources changes from time to time, so the emission factor for KSEB electricity is not fixed and will vary. The factor used on this site (about 0.71 t CO₂ per MWh) is therefore an assumed value, and the CO₂-avoided estimates will change when it is updated to the latest published factor.
  • Water-related figures use a grid emission factor of about 0.71 t CO₂ per MWh. The latest CEA baseline factor, the same one applied to solar, will be used when actual data is added.
  • The sewage treatment plant uses electricity itself, so its net saving is lower and could be close to zero. Avoided methane from untreated waste water is not included.
  • Burning biogas is counted as zero CO₂. Avoided methane from the digested waste is not included.
  • The ranges will be replaced with measured data: annual solar generation (kWh), waste fed to the bio-gas plant and gas use, treated water reused, and annual rainwater harvested.
