What to send

  • New or corrected activities: title, date, department or club, the SDGs it supports, a short description, and the number of beneficiaries and volunteer hours if known.
  • Electricity data: monthly solar generation for each plant (South Block, Auditorium, Administration Block), KSEB import and export units.
  • Photographs: original, unedited images with the facility name and the date taken.
  • Reports and documents: PDFs such as annual SDG reports or evidence records.
  • Corrections: the page, what is wrong, and the correct figure with its source.

Good to know

  • Please attach evidence (a bill, meter reading, circular or news link) for any figure, so the site stays accurate for accreditation reviews.
  • Updates are normally published within a few working days.
  • For the college's admissions and general enquiries, use the main website at vidyaacademy.ac.in.
